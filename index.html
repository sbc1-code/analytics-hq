How it works

Each property is tagged with its business purpose and the high-intent paths that matter, so traffic reads as customer-need signals instead of vanity metrics. The pipeline runs unattended: there is no dashboard tool to log into and no manual export.

Step 1FetchGA4 Data API, per property
Step 2AnalyzeWoW deltas, intent paths
Step 3AlertThreshold checks
Step 4ReportStatic HTML render
Step 5PublishGitHub Pages, on schedule
